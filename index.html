About Our Farm

Combining Family Farming with Professional Veterinary Care

Our family farm brings together poultry and dairy farming with a commitment to animal health and responsible farm management. We aim to care for our animals, maintain good farming practices, and provide reliable agricultural services.

Our veterinary work focuses on poultry management, cattle medicine, and animal health consultation, helping farmers make informed decisions about the health and management of their animals.
