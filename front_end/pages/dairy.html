Our Approach

Responsible Dairy Farm Management

Dairy farming involves attention to cattle nutrition, clean drinking water, suitable housing, hygiene, and regular monitoring of animal health.

Good management practices help support animal welfare, milk quality, and the long-term health of a dairy herd.

Caring for Our Cattle

Important Areas of Cattle Care

Healthy cattle depend on appropriate nutrition, good management, and timely veterinary attention.

🌾

Nutrition and Water

Suitable feed and continuous access to clean water are important for cattle health and milk production.

🐄

Health Monitoring

Observing cattle for changes in appetite, behavior, movement, and milk production can help identify concerns that need attention.

🧼

Hygiene and Welfare

Clean facilities, suitable living conditions, and careful milk handling support animal welfare and dairy hygiene.
